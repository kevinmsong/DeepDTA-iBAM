# IEEE Access template provenance

The main manuscript uses the official `ieeeaccess` author class. The supplement
uses a separate article layout with supplementary numbering.

- Author instructions: https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/
- Official template archive: https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ACCESS_latex_template_20260513-1-1.zip
- Retrieved: September 26, 2026
- Archive SHA-256: `60c7efc9db8ac9e8bdb31c550ad4e03cb6f258a878ececc0bc690b6203e45a67`

The class, bibliography style, supporting font files, and branding images are
unchanged from that archive. `template_assets.json` records their individual
hashes. These third-party template assets retain their original ownership and
terms; the repository's MIT license does not relicense them.

Submission-specific adjustments are confined to the manuscript preamble:
unassigned publication identifiers are blank, the footer reads "Submission
manuscript," bounded boxes reserve room for the abstract bullets and footer,
and font aliases and reference anchors resolve template compatibility issues.
The official page geometry, typography, headings, and branding are retained.
