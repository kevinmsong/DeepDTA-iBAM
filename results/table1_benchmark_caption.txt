\textbf{Standard KIBA split benchmark comparison.} The DeepDTA-iBAM row reports evaluation of the final trained integrated model on the canonical KIBA test partition. Literature rows are restricted to primary-source, regression-only standard-split results reporting concordance index (CI) and mean squared error (MSE), ensuring task-compatible comparison. Values are reproduced directly from the cited studies. For the local model, MSE is derived as RMSE$^2$ and both RMSE and MAE are retained in the narrative text because they are directly available from the present evaluation.
