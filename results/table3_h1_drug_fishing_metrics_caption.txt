Supplementary H1 retrieval stress-test metrics. Twenty curated H1 ligands were embedded into 5 independently sampled 10,000-compound lead-like libraries and ranked by DeepDTA-iBAM or by a nearest-active ECFP/Tanimoto baseline. Reported retrieval values are means across replicate libraries. Specificity mean reciprocal rank is provided only for the target-conditioned model because the similarity baseline does not define a comparable cross-target ranking.
