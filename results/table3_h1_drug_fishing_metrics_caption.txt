\textbf{External H1 retrieval test metrics.} The analysis used 20 curated H1 ligands embedded into 5 independently sampled 1000-compound lead-like mixed libraries drawn from ZINC22. Reported retrieval metrics are mean $\pm$ SD across replicate libraries. Because H1 lies outside the kinase-focused KIBA training distribution, these results characterize out-of-domain retrieval performance rather than prospective screening accuracy.
