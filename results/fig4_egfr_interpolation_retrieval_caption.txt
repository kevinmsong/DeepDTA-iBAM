Interpolation-guided EGFR ligand retrieval from a local chemical library. Six diverse EGFR-family anchor binders defined a minimum-spanning interpolation path in the target-conditioned ligand latent space. The left panel shows the projected latent-space arrangement of anchors, holdout EGFR-family binders, background compounds from a property-filtered lead-like ZINC tranche library, and the top ZINC hits selected by the combined path-plus-affinity score. The right panel shows cumulative recovery of holdout EGFR-family binders ranked against 2,000 ZINC compounds. Interpolation-path proximity outperformed the latent nearest-anchor baseline but remained weaker than explicit affinity and ligand-similarity ranking, so the result is best interpreted as a complementary retrieval signal rather than a stand-alone screening strategy.
