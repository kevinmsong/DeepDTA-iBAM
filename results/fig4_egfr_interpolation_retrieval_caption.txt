\textbf{Interpolation-guided EGFR ligand retrieval from a large chemical library.} Six diverse EGFR-family anchor binders defined a minimum-spanning interpolation path in the target-conditioned ligand latent space. Left: PCA projection showing anchors, holdout EGFR-family binders, background compounds from a local property-filtered lead-like ZINC tranche library, and the top ZINC hits selected by the combined path-plus-affinity score. Right: cumulative recovery of holdout EGFR-family binders ranked against 2000 ZINC compounds. Interpolation-path proximity outperformed the nearest-anchor baseline, whereas the explicit EGFR affinity head remained the strongest overall ranking signal, supporting interpolation-guided retrieval as a complementary target-conditioned lead-prioritization approach.
