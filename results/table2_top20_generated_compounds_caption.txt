\textbf{Top 20 EGFR-conditioned designed analogs from target-conditioned seeded molecular design.} Candidates were selected from 100 unique valid diffusion-decoded molecules and ranked in descending order of Lipinski compliance, alert-free status, quantitative estimate of drug-likeness, and predicted KIBA affinity, with synthetic accessibility score used as a penalty term (lower values preferred). Reported properties summarize the medicinal chemistry profile of each candidate together with its predicted EGFR-directed KIBA score.
