Residual diagnostics for the canonical standard-split KIBA test evaluation (n = 5,913). The left panel shows a Bland-Altman analysis of predicted and observed KIBA scores with the mean bias and 95 percent limits of agreement, and the right panel shows residuals plotted against the observed KIBA value to assess heteroscedasticity and systematic drift. The mean bias was -0.010, with limits of agreement from -0.895 to 0.876, indicating approximately centered errors but substantial single-point dispersion.
