\textbf{Interpolation-guided EGFR retrieval metrics.} Six EGFR-family anchor binders defined a piecewise linear interpolation path in the target-conditioned ligand latent space. Remaining EGFR-family holdouts (341) were ranked against 2000 compounds from a local property-filtered lead-like ZINC tranche library using four ranking schemes: interpolation-path proximity, nearest-anchor proximity, predicted EGFR affinity, or a combined path-plus-affinity score.
