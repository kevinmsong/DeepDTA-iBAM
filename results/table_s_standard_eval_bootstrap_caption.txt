Bootstrap uncertainty for the single-checkpoint standard-split evaluation. Metrics were recomputed over 600 bootstrap resamples of the held-out prediction pairs (n = 5,913) to obtain nonparametric 95 percent confidence intervals for RMSE, MAE, CI, Pearson correlation, and the coefficient of determination. These intervals quantify local evaluation uncertainty for the reported DeepDTA-iBAM row and should be interpreted separately from between-paper differences in the contextual benchmark table.
