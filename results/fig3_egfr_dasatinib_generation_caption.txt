\textbf{Target-conditioned seeded molecular design around dasatinib using the diffusion auxiliary head.} Repeated stochastic diffusion draws with low-noise perturbations were decoded under a fixed dasatinib graph topology until 100 unique valid designed analogs were obtained. The displayed molecules are the top 20 candidates after ranking by Lipinski compliance, alert-free status, quantitative estimate of drug-likeness, synthetic accessibility score, and predicted KIBA affinity against EGFR. This figure summarizes hypothesis-generating molecular candidates from a target-conditioned local exploration of the dasatinib scaffold neighborhood rather than unconstrained de novo generation.
