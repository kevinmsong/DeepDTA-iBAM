EGFR retrieval benchmark with explicit latent and ligand-only baselines. Six EGFR-family anchor binders defined the latent interpolation path, and the remaining 341 EGFR-family holdouts were ranked against 2,000 lead-like ZINC decoys. Reported metrics include AUROC, AUPRC, BEDROC20, enrichment factors, and recovery at fixed ranked fractions, with bootstrap confidence intervals obtained by resampling the candidate set. The primary table focuses on the core ranking methods, whereas the exploratory combined score is deferred to the supplement to limit circularity.
